Narrative Memory Momentum is a paper-trading Playbook for liquid crypto perpetual markets. It tries to capture the period when a crypto narrative is turning into tradable momentum, while still having a fallback scanner for ordinary market opportunities when no narrative is active. This is the strategy section (策略) for subscribers who want to understand the behavior before trusting the signals.

Strategy overview: the Playbook treats AI coins, RWA tokenization, meme-cycle flow, and DePIN as narrative families. In historical backtests, live news and social feeds cannot be replayed fairly, so narrative detection is implemented as deterministic market proxies and a small memory map of how each narrative type has historically behaved. The agent looks for theme-linked strength, volume confirmation, and broad market tone. If the narrative score is not strong enough, it falls back to three scanner rules: momentum long, fear bounce, and volume breakout.

Opening positions (开仓): the strategy opens only paper long positions. A long can be opened when the active narrative overlay agrees with price and volume momentum, or when the fallback scanner finds a configured symbol that is rising with participation, recovering after a fearful market drop, or breaking out on unusual volume with positive price action. The scanner chooses the highest-ranked symbol from the configured universe.

Closing positions (平仓): every paper trade uses a defensive stop loss and a take profit target. The stop loss is fixed from configuration, while the take profit target is scaled by narrative memory. Stronger remembered narratives allow a larger objective; weaker or fallback regimes use a more conservative target. The strategy closes the open paper position when either exit condition is reached.

Risk (风险): this Playbook can lose money in choppy, sideways markets, false breakouts, late-stage narratives, sudden news reversals, and thin-liquidity conditions. Narrative memory is a heuristic overlay, not a guarantee that a current story will behave like a past one. Backtest metrics should be read as evidence about this deterministic paper-trading implementation, not as a promise of live returns.

User-tunable parameters include trading symbols, narrative mode, leverage, margin budget, momentum sensitivity, fear-bounce sensitivity, and breakout-volume sensitivity. Raising leverage increases both gains and drawdowns. Expanding the symbol universe can find more trades but may also add noisier markets. Increasing sensitivity makes the Playbook react sooner but can increase false positives.
